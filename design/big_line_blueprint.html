Big line blueprint

Five big jumps down a steep hillside. The slope gives you the speed, so nobody needs to pedal. Each landing is built tall and long so there's a usable speed window. A big berm and a run-out finish the line. Built in the game (press 3); these are the built dimensions.

Each drawing shows the flight at the slowest and fastest speed that still lands on the landing. Any slower and you case the landing's face; any faster and you fly past the bottom onto the hill.

Speed window: lip speeds that, with a pop, touch down on the landing between the knuckle and the start of its curve into the trail, within 20° of the slope. Worked out by flying the rider (with air drag) over this exact profile at every km/h, not from a formula.
