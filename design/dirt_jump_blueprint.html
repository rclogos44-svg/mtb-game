Dirt jump park blueprint

Three lines off one start hill: a pro line with a table, a double, a step-up and a step-down; a beginner line; transfers between the two in both directions; and a flow line back through rollers and a hip. Three berms link them. Built in the game (press 3 or 4); dimensions here match the game, tuned so each line rides with pedalling between jumps.
